---
title: Hardware Semantics & Driver Model
description: Single-driver rules, capability tokens, and deterministic elaboration.
---

In Syl, `cell` bodies describe structural hardware connectivity rather than imperative control flow:

- **Continuous assignment (`:=`)** binds a signal to a single combinational expression.
- **Sequential registers (`reg ... reset(...)` + `next`)** bind state updates to a specific `Clock<D>` and `Reset<D, ...>`.
- **Memories (`mem`)** use explicit `mem_write` and `mem_read_sync` / combinational indexing primitives.

During elaboration, `syl_elab` builds driver facts for every net and verifies completeness and non-overlap before lowering to `syl_hw`.
