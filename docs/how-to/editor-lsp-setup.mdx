`syl_lsp` provides fast, Salsa-backed IDE features with an isolated, cancellable background elaboration DRC channel.

## Supported Capabilities

- **Incremental Sync**: `TextDocumentSyncKind::INCREMENTAL` with UTF-16 position mapping.
- **Project Reload**: Automatic `syl.toml` discovery and `didChangeWatchedFiles` hot reload.
- **Fast Path vs. Background DRC**: Parse/HIR/TIR diagnostics run on the fast path; full elaboration DRC runs asynchronously at low priority and cancels cleanly on new edits.
- **Navigation & Refactoring**: `find_references`, `prepare_rename` / `rename`, `workspace/symbol`.
- **Rich Assistance**: `inlayHint` (inferred types, widths, clock domains), `semanticTokens`, `signatureHelp`.

## Point Your Editor to `syl_lsp`

Configure your LSP client (VS Code, Neovim, Helix, Zed) to launch `syl_lsp` over stdio for `*.syl` files.
